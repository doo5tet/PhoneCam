PhoneCam

Turn your phone into a camera for Blender

Scan the QR code in Blender

In Blender, press N in the 3D Viewport, open the PhoneCam tab, click Start and scan the QR code with your phone's camera.

Checking the connection…

One-time setup (about 1 minute). Phones only share their motion sensors with secure pages, so your phone needs to trust your own computer once.

1Download the certificate
Download certificate

Safari asks to allow a configuration profile: tap Allow, then Close.

2Install it
  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Profile Downloaded near the top.
  3. Tap Install, enter your passcode, Install again, then Done.
3Trust it
  1. Settings → General → About.
  2. Scroll down → Certificate Trust Settings.
  3. Switch on PhoneCam on … (your computer) → Continue.
1Download the certificate
Download certificate
2Install it as a CA certificate
  1. Open Settings and search for “CA certificate”.
  2. Tap Install a certificate → CA certificate → Install anyway.
  3. Pick PhoneCam-CA.crt from Downloads.

Menu names differ a little between phone brands (often under Security → Encryption & credentials).

4Open the camera

This page notices by itself when your phone trusts the computer and opens the camera.
