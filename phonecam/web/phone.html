How to film

  • Move your phone to aim the camera. ⊙ resets the aim.
  • WALK stick moves the camera; UP / DOWN raise or lower it.
  • Zoom: tap .5 · 1× · 2 · 3 · 5, pinch the picture, or slide along the lens buttons.
  • CINEMATIC blurs the background — tap the picture to choose what is sharp. FISHEYE is super wide.
  • Practice plays the shot without recording. The red button records; the video saves to your computer.
  • After a take: Watch, Retake or Save video.
  • Camera spots (top) jump to the cameras in your Blender scene.
  • ⚙ Rig: Handheld, Dolly, Crane, Cinematic or Raw · Tripod (rotation only) · Sensitivity, Smoothing, Position scale · keyframe rate · AR position tracking.
